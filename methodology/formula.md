# COLA calculation methodology

The Social Security COLA is based on the Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers (CPI-W), BLS series `CWUR0000SA0`.

For the 2027 benefit year:

1. Average the July, August, and September 2026 CPI-W readings.
2. Compare that average with the applicable 2025 third-quarter CPI-W baseline.
3. Calculate the percentage increase.
4. Round the percentage to the nearest one-tenth of one percent according to the statutory method.
5. If the result is zero or negative, no COLA is payable.

```text
COLA = ((2026 Q3 average − 2025 Q3 baseline) / 2025 Q3 baseline) × 100
```

Until all three 2026 readings are published, COLA Insight labels the displayed percentage as a forecast. Once all readings exist, it may be labeled computed while SSA confirmation remains pending. Only SSA can publish the official COLA.
