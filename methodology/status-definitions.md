# Status definitions

## Forecast

An independent estimate used while one or more required Q3 CPI-W observations are unavailable. It is not an SSA announcement.

## Computed

All three required CPI-W observations have been published and the statutory calculation can be reproduced. SSA confirmation is still pending.

## Official

The Social Security Administration has published the COLA for the applicable benefit year.

Older records remain in the snapshot history when the status changes. Corrections are appended or explained; prior forecasts are not silently rewritten.
